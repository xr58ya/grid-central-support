# Grid Central training guide

A complete, step-by-step guide to everything Grid Central does and how to set it up. Start with chapter 1 if you're new, or jump straight to the feature you need.

| Chapter | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [1. Getting started](01-getting-started.md) | Installing, your beta code, the companion, the setup wizard, the Overview, your account and the plans, every Settings tab, backup, updates and troubleshooting |
| [2. Spotter and coach](02-spotter-and-coach.md) | Live data, the AI spotter and its calls, the radar, the track map, corner-by-corner coaching, lap analysis, the race engineer, rivals and your ratings |
| [3. Data, strategy and setups](03-data-strategy-setups.md) | Lap data and comparisons, .ibt telemetry, the session debrief, the race planner, live strategy, pit fuel, tyres and weather, team races and car setups |
| [4. Voice and phone](04-voice-and-phone.md) | Talkback and every voice command, choosing voices, stream commentary and the phone pit wall |
| [5. Overlays, streaming and team racing](05-overlays-stream-team.md) | The overlay designer and every widget, OBS, the in-game overlay, Twitch and YouTube, Discord, the league room and the team hub |
| [6. Paint shop](06-paint-shop.md) | Your first livery in 10 steps, every paint tool, getting your paint into iRacing and the Spray Booth |

**During the beta, every feature is unlocked.** Where the guide mentions a plan (Rookie, Driver, Race Team or the Broadcast add-on), that describes how Grid Central will work once it goes on sale.

This guide is written for the version on the development branch, so a few things may be newer than the version you have. Your version is shown at the bottom right of the app, for example "V 0.5.9.1 · BETA".

Something missing, wrong or unclear? [Open a setup help ticket](https://github.com/xr58ya/grid-central-support/issues/new?template=setup_help.yml) or email support.gridcentral@gmail.com.
