Privacy

What we keep when you send us a report, a feature idea or a question for setup help, and why.

What we keep

  • Your answers to the form, such as your Grid Central version, what happened and how to make it happen again.
  • Your email address, so we can send you your ticket number and reply to you.
  • Files you attach, such as companion.log, screenshots or a short video. A companion.log can show your Windows user name (in file paths) and the names of other drivers in your sessions.
  • The ticket's history: our replies to you, our notes, its status and when each email was sent.

We don't ask for your name, and we don't use your report for advertising.

Why

Only to understand the problem or idea, fix Grid Central, and reply to you. Your answers and files are private: only the Grid Central support team can see them. Nothing you send here is posted publicly.

Who handles it for us

  • Cloudflare runs this site and stores tickets and files. Its Turnstile check, the "are you human" box on the form, looks at your browser and connection to keep out spam.
  • Resend sends our emails to you.

How long we keep it

As long as it helps us fix and improve Grid Central. If you'd like to see what you sent, or have it deleted, email support.gridcentral@gmail.com with your ticket number (for example GC-1001) from the address you used.

Never send passwords, payment details or your iRacing login, in a report or an email. We will never ask for them.

Contact

Questions about your data or anything else: support.gridcentral@gmail.com.
